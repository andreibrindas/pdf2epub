- Keep Romanian diacritics exactly as printed, including the old cedilla forms ş ţ Ş Ţ (the
  build normalises them to ș ț). Watch ă/â/î/ş/ţ carefully against the image; OCR drops and
  swaps them.
- Real hyphens are very common in Romanian: clitics and compounds such as `s-a`, `n-a`,
  `într-o`, `dintr-un`, `de-a`, `l-a`, `i-au`, `într-adevăr`, `moral-cognitivă`. When a line or
  page ends in a hyphen, decide from the language whether it is one of these (keep it) or a
  word split (join it).
- Quotes as printed: opening „ (U+201E), closing ” (U+201D); inner quotes « ». OCR often gives
  “ or " for the closing quote: write ”. Apostrophes ’. Never straight " or '.
- Dashes as printed: — or – used as punctuation stay as printed.
- Ellipsis as printed: "..." for three dots.
