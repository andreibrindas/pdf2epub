- Quotes as printed, typographic: “ ” for double, ‘ ’ for single, ’ for apostrophes. Never
  straight " or '.
- Dashes as printed: an em dash — (keep spaces around it only if the print clearly has them).
  Hyphen-minus for ranges like "pp. 58-90" as printed.
- Ellipsis as printed ("..." or ". . ."): use "..." for either.
